3D-printed tiles (two colours)

Each tile is two STL files: _white (body, 2.4 mm + white cells) and _black (black cells, top 0.6 mm, flush). In Bambu Studio / OrcaSlicer / PrusaSlicer import both files at once and answer Yes to "load as a single object with multiple parts", then give the black part black filament. Use matte filament (gloss reflects and hides the pattern), 0.2 mm layers, 100% top infill. The small tab sticks out of the FRONT edge.

  • ID 10 (4 in): white + black — 127 × 135 mm
  • ID 20 (6 in): white + black — 191 × 202 mm (needs a 256 mm bed)
  • IDs 21–23: 21 white / black, 22 white / black, 23 white / black
