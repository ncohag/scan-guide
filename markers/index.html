3D-printed tiles (two colours)

Each tile is two STL files: _white (body, 2.4 mm + white cells) and _black (black cells, top 0.6 mm, flush). In Bambu Studio / OrcaSlicer / PrusaSlicer import both files at once and answer Yes to "load as a single object with multiple parts", then give the black part black filament. Use matte filament (gloss reflects and hides the pattern), 0.2 mm layers, 100% top infill. The small tab sticks out of the FRONT edge.

  • ID 10 (4 in): white + black — 127 × 135 mm
  • ID 20 (6 in): white + black — 191 × 202 mm (needs a 256 mm bed)
  • IDs 21–23: 21 white / black, 22 white / black, 23 white / black

Turntable (small objects)

Phone on a tripod, object in the middle, plain background behind it; turn the platter by hand and the app shoots every new 15° step when it stops. Platter: 200 mm, 12 tags (IDs 40–51, 24 mm) on an 80 mm radius, spins on a standard 608 skateboard bearing (8 × 22 × 7 mm) over a printed base with an 8 mm pin. Print the platter top-up (the bearing pocket underneath bridges fine) and the base flat.

  • platter white + platter black (tags are raised 0.6 mm in black)
  • base (one colour)
  • Try it first on paper: turntable_top_paper.pdf — cut out the circle, glue it on any lazy susan.

Scan cube (scale in any scan)

40 mm cube with 32 mm tags on five faces (top 30, front 31, right 32, back 33, left 34) so one is always readable, even from low angles. Set it beside the object; the PC pipeline uses it for scale. white + black (the black cells go 4 mm deep, flush with the faces; print upright, no supports).
