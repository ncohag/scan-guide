Sensors

Motion and camera access are needed for live aiming. Without them you still get the shot list.

Calibrate

Stand behind the object and point the back camera along its front direction: for a cannon, sight along the barrel toward the muzzle, phone level.

Everything after this is measured from that direction, so no compass bearings are needed. Recalibrate if the guidance starts to drift.
